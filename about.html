About

Thanthai is a painter and sculptor based in Bangkok.

Born in 2000 in Bangkok, Thailand, graduated from Ladkrabang University in 2023 with a bachelor’s degree in Fine Art.

Deeply influenced by the nostalgia of the early 2000s digital awakening, Nachai’s work reflects a captivating blend of his personal experiences and interests.
